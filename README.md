# Bio × AI Portfolio

## 1. Introduction

This repository is a Bio × AI portfolio created to demonstrate my preparation for the SKALA 4th program.

I have a strong background in biology, biomedical science, and wet-lab research. However, my technical skills in AI and software development are still in the learning and growth stage.

To bridge this gap, I created this portfolio to show how I can use generative AI tools, GitHub, and vibe coding to turn biological and biomedical problems into practical AI-based service ideas.

## 4. Why Bio × AI?

I believe that AI becomes more valuable when it is connected to real domain problems.

In the biomedical field, there are many complex problems that require both biological understanding and computational thinking, such as:

- Interpreting experimental results
- Organizing wet-lab protocols

Through this portfolio, I aim to show how my biology-based background can be connected with generative AI and service development.

## 5. Project List

### Project 1. Bio Paper Summarizer

Bio Paper Summarizer is a generative AI-based tool that summarizes biomedical papers into structured sections.

The goal of this project is to help users understand complex biomedical papers more efficiently.

Planned summary sections include:

- One-sentence summary
- Research background
- Research purpose
- Experimental methods
- Key results
- Limitations
- Future research ideas
- Beginner-friendly explanation

Status: Planning / Prototype

---

### Project 2. Wet-lab Protocol Assistant

Wet-lab Protocol Assistant is an AI assistant designed to help researchers organize experimental protocols and identify possible troubleshooting points.

This project is based on the idea that wet-lab researchers often need to check experimental steps, prepare reagents, and respond to unexpected problems during experiments.

Planned features include:

- Protocol step organization
- Experiment checklist generation
- Troubleshooting suggestions
- Beginner-friendly protocol explanation
- Experimental caution points

Status: Planning

---

### Project 3. Biomedical Keyword Chatbot

Biomedical Keyword Chatbot is a chatbot that explains biomedical and AI-related keywords at different levels.

The goal of this project is to help users understand complex concepts in a more structured and accessible way.

Planned explanation levels include:

- Beginner level
- University student level
- Biomedical researcher level
- Job interview preparation level

Example keywords:

- ADC
- GLP-1
- mRNA vaccine
- Targeted protein degradation
- Biomarker
- LLM
- Transformer
- Prompt engineering

Status: Planning

---

### Project 4. Bio-AI Drug Discovery Platform Concept

Bio-AI Drug Discovery Platform Concept is an AI-based drug discovery platform idea designed to connect biomedical knowledge, literature analysis, target discovery, biomarker interpretation, and candidate prioritization.

This project is currently in the ideation stage and aims to explore how generative AI can support the early stages of new drug development.

Planned platform modules include:

1. Disease & Target Research Module  
   - Organizes disease-related genes, proteins, pathways, and mechanisms

2. Paper Intelligence Module  
   - Summarizes biomedical papers and extracts key evidence

3. Biomarker Interpretation Module  
   - Helps interpret the relationship between biomarkers and treatment response

4. Candidate Prioritization Module  
   - Supports the prioritization of targets or candidate compounds

5. Report Generator Module  
   - Converts analysis results into a structured research report

Status: Concept / Planning
