# DTSR Scoring Framework

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the DTSR scoring framework is to prioritize potential drug development targets based on disease biology, targetability, safety, modality fit, and supporting evidence.

DTSR does not aim to simply list disease-associated biomarkers.  
Instead, it aims to evaluate whether a biomarker, gene, or protein may be developed into an actionable therapeutic target.

---

## 2. Data Access Principle

DTSR follows a public API-based data access principle.

DTSR analyzes publicly accessible biomedical information, including:

- Paper metadata
- Abstracts
- Open-access full text when available
- Public biological databases
- Disease-target association data
- Protein and pathway information
- Normal tissue expression data

DTSR does not collect or analyze paywalled full-text papers without permission.

The results generated by DTSR should be interpreted as target candidate prioritization, not as a definitive conclusion.

---

## 3. Scoring Categories

DTSR evaluates each target candidate using seven categories.

Each category is scored from 1 to 5.

DTSR Score = Disease Relevance
